About

XiangyunHu (胡翔耘) - PhD student at the Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences

XiangyunHu胡翔耘

I am a PhD student at the Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences. I received my undergraduate training in Intelligent Sensing Engineering at Southeast University.

My research interests lie in visual intelligence and multimodal learning. My previous work has explored visual perception and decision-making for autonomous driving, as well as physical simulation for additive manufacturing.

01 / Research

I am interested in how visual and multimodal systems perceive complex environments and support reliable decisions. These are my current research interests and directions for future doctoral work.

02 / Selected work

Visual perception & decision-making · 2024–2026

Vision-based perception and intelligent decision-making for autonomous driving

Developed a perception-to-decision pipeline using multi-resolution stereo matching and online parameter updates for disparity estimation. The decision-making work explored a mixture-of-experts model and diffusion-based sample generation for long-tail scenarios. The national undergraduate innovation project received an excellent completion evaluation.

Physical simulation · 2024–2025

Warping simulation for FDM 3D printing

Developed a coupled thermal-mechanical model based on mass-spring dynamics and finite differences to simulate shrinkage and warping during printing. The delivered project reported a mean error of 0.04 mm on its simulation test set.

03 / Additional research

Personalized upper-limb rehabilitation trajectories

Ongoing work on a trajectory generation system combining a Transformer-based imitation model and a diffusion generator. Initial experiments are complete; data collection and model refinement are in progress.
